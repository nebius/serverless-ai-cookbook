# OpenFold2 NIM (hosted, bounded)

Use `bionemo_openfold2` for one protein sequence of at most 1000 residues, with
optional alignments/templates. Select at most two models for an event request.
Report pLDDT/pTM or available confidence fields with their limitations. Return
PDB/mmCIF and JSON artifacts and require experimental validation.
